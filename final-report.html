The twist

The requisition-chains flag wasn't even the real problem. Cross‑checking it against Halloran's own interview showed the brief's stated problem — "needs a second approval, more oversight on big purchases" — contradicts what Halloran actually said: a single approval already took 11 days on an urgent repair, with a broken priority queue. A second sign‑off would make his real complaint worse, not better. No checklist item catches that alone — it took reading the source. Now flagged for Helen before any 4.3 scope call.
